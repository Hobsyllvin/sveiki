**Scope:** interlinear decoding only (lemma / gloss / pos / note / tokenization / decode-vs-`natural`).
Quality and naturalness of the Latvian itself is out of scope. 45 sentences, 293 tokens, 10 sections.

## What passed

Mechanical layer is clean, verified programmatically:

- **Reconstruction:** 0 failures. `lv + (punct ?? "")` joined on single spaces reproduces `target` exactly for all 45 sentences, including the em-dash break in s43 (`saku` → `punct: " —"` with the required leading space).
- **Tokenization:** nothing merged, split, or dropped; no punctuation leaked into `lv` or `gloss`; no spaces inside glosses.
- **Dictionary conformance (R13):** every one of the 119 distinct lemmas used exists in `dictionary.json`, and every gloss used is in that lemma's approved list. No out-of-list prepositional glosses (R8).
- **Lemma bookkeeping:** the lesson's `newLemmas` is byte-identical to `course.json`'s; nothing declared-new is unused; nothing used is undeclared; no re-declaration of prior-lesson vocabulary.
- **Morphology:** all 60+ inflected verb forms are filed under the correct infinitive, checked individually — including the traps: `nogaršot` correctly kept *separate* from `garšot` rather than conflated, `atnes` → `atnest`, `iesaku` → `ieteikt`, `satiekas` → `satikties`, and the three adv/adj homograph pairs correctly split by syntax (`tā` adv vs `tas` demonstr. in s30/s13, `jauki` adv vs `jauks` adj in s26/s29, `lieliski` adv vs `lielisks` adj in s38/s42, `labi` adv vs `labs` adj in s33/s44). Case assignment on all genitive modifiers, locatives, and dative experiencers is correct.
- s36's `kūkas` note (`"pl.; case ambiguous (elliptical)"`) is exactly the right kind of honest annotation.

`npm run validate` passes — but note it prints `morphology: skipped — Tēzaurs/analyzer integration pending`, so **no lemma or form claim in this lesson is machine-verified**. Everything in the "What passed" list above was checked by hand or by ad-hoc script for this review only; CI would not have caught any of it.

## Systemic patterns

1. **The decode line leaks meaning into `note` for oblique cases the rules don't cover.** R4 grants `to-` only to dative *experiencers*, so the dative of purpose in s27 degrades to a bare `joy` and the sentence stops being decodable (the single error below). This is a rules gap, not a decoder slip.
2. **Four glossing conventions were invented in `dictionary.json` notes rather than in the rules doc**, contrary to the standing instruction *"Never invent a gloss convention; if a rule is missing, add it to the rules doc first."* Affected: diminutives (`pīlīte` — its own note admits "No rule covers diminutives yet"), reciprocal reflexives (`satikties`), dative of purpose (`priekam`), and locative absorption under an attributive adjective (`brīvajā laikā` → `free in-time`).
3. **Note granularity is inconsistent across sibling tokens** — nominative `tu` is always marked, nominative `es` never is; some genitives state number, others don't; some adjectives state gender, others don't; one `ir` gets a construction gloss, its twin doesn't. Individually trivial, collectively they make the notes unreliable as a signal of what the decoder actually recognised.
4. **`natural` fields drift from the decode line in six places** — a dropped intensifier, a flipped person, and four idiomatic word choices that quietly erase distinctions the dictionary works to establish (`braukt` vs `iet`, `kāds` vs `kurš`, `labdien` vs `sveiks`).
5. **Three dictionary entries store a case-bound form as the lemma's only gloss**, breaking lemma-level identity and setting up a future validator rejection.
