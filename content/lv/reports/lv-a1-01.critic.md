# Critic report — lv-a1-01 (`Mani sauc… (Introductions and numbers)`)

Adversarial decoding review against `content/_shared/GLOSSING_RULES.md` R1–R15 + the punctuation
convention, and `content/lv/dictionary.json`. Scope: decoding only (lemma / gloss / pos / note /
tokenization / gloss-line-vs-`natural`). The Latvian sentences themselves were not judged.

**Reviewed:** 20 sentences, 4 sections, 108 tokens.
**Result:** 1 error, 13 warnings.

## What is solid (so the reader knows where the review actually looked)

- **Tokenization is exact.** All 20 sentences reconstruct from `lv + (punct ?? "")` joined with
  single spaces and equal `target` character-for-character. Nothing merged, split, dropped, or
  double-punctuated. Word count equals token count in every sentence, including the 11- and
  14-token phone-number sentences (s19, s20).
- **Every lemma is correct.** All 24 distinct inflected forms map to the true citation form:
  `Mani`/`tevi`/`tev` → `es`/`tu` (not treated as separate lemmas), `sauc` → `saukt`,
  `esi`/`esmu`/`ir` → `būt`, `mēnešus` → `mēnesis`, `mācos` → `mācīties`, `saprotu` → `saprast`,
  `redzēšanos` → `redzēšanās`, `Latvijas`/`Lietuvas` → `Latvija`/`Lietuva`.
- **R15's syncretism trap is avoided.** `studente` (s17) is filed under the feminine lemma
  `studente`, not as an inflected form of `students`; `skolotājs` (s16) carries `masc.`
- **Case marking is complete.** Every locative (`Rīgā` ×2, `pilsētā`) absorbs to `in-` per R4 and
  is noted `loc.`; every genitive (`Latvijas`, `Lietuvas`, `telefona`) is bare-glossed with the
  case in `note` per R4/R12; every accusative (`Mani`, `tevi`, `mēnešus`, `redzēšanos`) is noted.
- **R14 is not violated by omission** — this lesson contains no address-by-name, so no vocative
  was missed.
- All `audioApproved` flags are `false`, per the standing instruction.
- Verb person labels are all accurate, including the two that look wrong but are not:
  `tu dzīvo` (s11) and `tu runā` (s14) genuinely are 2sg present.

## Cross-cutting patterns

1. **Sense selection is not being checked against dictionary notes.** The single error below is a
   token glossed with the wrong one of two approved senses, where `dictionary.json` names that
   exact phrase and prescribes the other. Nothing in the pipeline enforces the sense-selection
   half of R13 — only membership in the gloss list. Worth a validator rule.
2. **Notes explaining a construction appear once and then vanish** — even when the *next*
   occurrence is a structurally different case. `sauc` in s1 explains the impersonal
   `mani sauc`; s2 turns it into a 2nd-person question and drops the explanation (finding 2).
3. **`natural` sometimes lands too far from the decode line to be bridgeable** (findings 3, 4).
   R9 rightly keeps the decode literal, but when `natural` also idiomatises away from the gloss,
   the learner loses both ends of the rope.
4. **Nominative is marked inconsistently** — `prieks`, `numurs`, `Jānis`, `Emma` get `nom.`;
   `es`, `tu`, `Es` get nothing; `mans`, `tavs`, `kāds` get `masc. nom.` No rule covers this
   (finding 12).
5. **Adverb glossing has no documented policy** — `labi` (adv) → adverbial `well`, but `jauki`
   (adv) → adjectival `nice`. The decision lives only in dictionary notes, not in the rules doc
   (finding 13), contrary to the standing instruction that missing conventions go in the rules
   doc first.

## Notes for the human reviewer

- The one error (s11 `Cik`) is a **passing-validator** error: both candidate glosses are in the
  approved list, so only a human or a sense-aware check catches it. If lv-a1-02/03 also select
  senses for multi-gloss lemmas (`kas`, `uz`, `vēl`, `tas`, `garšot`), the same class of error is
  likely there and is worth a targeted sweep.
- Findings 7, 12, 13 and the `pos` question in 6 are all requests to **write down an existing
  decision** in GLOSSING_RULES.md rather than changes to the lesson. They are warnings because
  the current data is defensible; they matter because the next lesson will guess differently.
- Findings 9, 10 and 12 touch `dictionary.json` / `course.json`, not the lesson tokens. Flagged
  here because they were discovered by this review; route them wherever dictionary changes go.
