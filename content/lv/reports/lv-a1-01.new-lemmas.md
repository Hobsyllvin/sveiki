# lv-a1-01 — new lemmas

Lesson: **Mani sauc… (Introductions and numbers)** — 20 sentences, 4 dialogue sections.

59 distinct lemmas used, of which **52 are new** to the course (7 carry over from lv-a1-00:
`es`, `tu`, `dzīvot`, `no`, `Rīga`, `uz`, `iet`).

Of the 52, **19 required a new `dictionary.json` entry** and are marked
`needsNativeReview: true`. The remaining 33 were already present in the dictionary from the
earlier pipeline run — their glosses were reused unchanged, as instructed.

## Gloss changes to existing entries

Two pre-existing entries were amended rather than added:

- **`es`** — added `me` to the approved gloss list. The accusative `mani` ("Mani sauc…",
  s1/s3) needs a gloss distinct from nominative `I` and dative `to-me`. Note updated to
  `man = dat. (to-me); mani = acc. (me)`.
- **`skolotājs`** — old note read *"masc. lemma by course convention; fem. skolotāja glossed
  the same"*, which R15 now contradicts. Split into two lemmas (`skolotājs` masc. /
  `skolotāja` fem.) cross-referenced with `seeAlso`. Same treatment applied to
  `students` / `studente`.
