**What is genuinely well done** (stated so the reviewer can see where scrutiny is *not* needed):
`Marta` is correctly nom. in s1 (`Mani sauc Marta`) but voc. in s2 (`Sveika, Marta!`) per R14;
the syncretic forms `dzīvo`, `strādā`, `runā` are correctly disambiguated 2sg vs 3 pres. throughout;
the genitive of negation (`brāļa`, s25) and genitive after `no` (`kuras valsts`, `Igaunijas`,
`Lietuvas`) are correctly marked; `Rīga` is glossed nom. `Riga` in s10 rather than being
over-absorbed to `in-Riga`, which is the trap R4 sets; the dative experiencer `man`/`tev` is
consistently `to-me`/`to-you` per R4.

**Systemic patterns.** Two of the issues below are not decoder slips but gaps in the contract:
R4 says nothing about locative NPs that contain a modifier (E1), and nothing distinguishes the
lexical `in-` of the `-iski` language adverbs from the locative `in-` that R4 mandates (W1).
Both will recur in every later lesson, so they are worth resolving in `GLOSSING_RULES.md`
before more content is generated.

| s29 | `brīvajā` + `laikā` | error | `Ko tu dari brīvajā laikā?` decodes to `what you do free in-time`. The locative spans the whole NP `brīvajā laikā`, but R4's `in-` is attached only to the head noun, so the preposition surfaces *after* its own modifier. A learner reading the decode line in order cannot recover "in free time" — they read two unrelated items, `free` and `in-time`. Each token is individually R4-compliant, so the defect is in the rule, not the token; but the rendered line loses the meaning R4 exists to preserve. | Amend R4 first (CLAUDE.md: "if a rule is missing, add it to the rules doc first"), then re-decode. Preferred: attach `in-` to the **leftmost** element of a locative NP → `brīvajā` = `in-free`, `laikā` = `time`. Alternative: keep `in-` on the head and add `note: "loc. NP: brīvajā laikā"` to `brīvajā`. Either way the decision must be recorded, as every adjective+locative phrase will hit it. |

## Summary

- **2 errors**, **8 warnings**, 0 reconstruction failures, 0 unapproved glosses, 0 missing dictionary entries.
- No token in this lesson has a wrong `lemma`, a wrong `gloss` for its inflected form, or a wrong `pos`.
  The morphological annotation is accurate throughout, including the traps (nom./voc. `Marta`,
  syncretic `dzīvo`/`strādā`, genitive of negation, nom. `Rīga` vs loc. `Rīgā`).
- The two errors are of different kinds and need different owners: **E1** is a gap in
  `GLOSSING_RULES.md` R4 that will recur in every lesson containing a modified locative NP and
  should be resolved in the rules doc before more content is generated; **E2** is a data-integrity
  desync between the lesson file and `course.json` that CI should be catching mechanically and
  currently is not.
- Of the warnings, **W1** (`-iski` adverbs vs locative `in-`) is the most pedagogically load-bearing
  and shares a root cause with E1: the decode line uses one `in-` prefix for two unrelated
  grammatical facts.
