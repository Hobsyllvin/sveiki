# lv-a1-01 — Mani sauc… (audio script)

Tagged delivery script for ElevenLabs Text to Dialogue (eleven_v3).
Format: `<sentenceId> | <Speaker> | <tagged Latvian text>`
The Latvian text is byte-identical to the lesson's `target`; only `[bracketed]`
tags are added. A tag must be followed by a word, never by punctuation.

A first meeting between strangers, so the register is polite and a little formal;
warmth builds as it goes. The phone numbers are tagged `[slowly]` — they are the
lesson's drill target and a learner has to catch every digit.

s1 | Jānis | [warmly] Labdien! Mani sauc Jānis.
s2 | Jānis | Kā tevi sauc?
s3 | Emma | [cheerfully] Sveiki! Mani sauc Emma.
s4 | Emma | Prieks iepazīties!
s5 | Jānis | [warmly] Prieks! Kā tev iet?
s6 | Emma | Ļoti labi, paldies. Un tev?
s7 | Jānis | Arī labi, paldies.
s8 | Jānis | Vai tu esi no Latvijas?
s9 | Emma | Nē, es esmu no Lietuvas.
s10 | Emma | Bet es dzīvoju šeit, Rīgā.
s11 | Jānis | [curious] Cik ilgi tu dzīvo Rīgā?
s12 | Emma | Es dzīvoju šeit desmit mēnešus.
s13 | Emma | [shyly] Es vēl mācos latviski.
s14 | Jānis | [impressed] Tu labi runā latviski!
s15 | Emma | [laughing] Paldies! Bet es saprotu tikai mazliet.
s16 | Jānis | Es esmu skolotājs. Es strādāju šeit, pilsētā.
s17 | Emma | [excited] Ļoti jauki! Es esmu studente.
s18 | Emma | Kāds ir tavs telefona numurs?
s19 | Jānis | [slowly] Mans numurs ir divi pieci, viens četri astoņi, nulle trīs deviņi.
s20 | Emma | [slowly] Un mans numurs ir divi seši, septiņi viens pieci, nulle trīs četri. [warmly] Uz redzēšanos!
