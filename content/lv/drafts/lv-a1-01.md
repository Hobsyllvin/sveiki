## The six fixes

1. **Stumble moved to Jānis** (s3–6). He attempts `prieks ie-pa-zī-ties` haltingly, calls it a hard word, gets it right, and Emma echoes it back. It now characterises the weaker speaker, as it should.
2. **"What do you work" → `Kur tu strādā?`** — "where do you work", the natural Latvian question.
3. **Want kept as the emotional beat, farewell after it** (s25–26). `Mums jāmācās kopā` lands first, then `Uz redzēšanos` / `Tiekamies!`
4. **`Tas ir daudz darba, tiešām`** — `tiešām` is the "indeed" you asked for.
5. **Line 3 is now `Es esmu Jānis`**, giving two ways to introduce yourself alongside Emma's `Mani sauc`.
6. **Numbering fixed** — 26 continuous lines.

Also kept: "many new pupils" — the teacher too busy to be a student is quiet, useful irony.

## Flags for the native reviewer

1. **`Tagad mums jārunā latviski`** — debitive with dative plural. Confirm a student would echo a teacher's instruction this way.
2. **`Darām!`** — present 1pl of `darīt` used as "let's do it". Confirm this is idiomatic and not too casual for a classroom. Fallback if it doesn't work: `Labi, es mēģinu` ("alright, I'll try", 1sg present). With this change the debitive is the only non-present form in the lesson.
3. **`ie-pa-zī-ties`** — is syllable-splitting written this way in Latvian, or is there another convention for showing a stumbled word?
4. **`Jāni!`** — vocative of Jānis. Confirm.
5. **`Mani vecāki ir latvieši`** — confirm nom. pl. noun rather than an adjective form.
6. **`Divas reizes nedēļā`** / **`vienu reizi nedēļā`** — confirm both forms.
7. **`Man vajag trīs reizes`** — does `vajag` work with a bare accusative here, or does it need an infinitive?
8. **`Tagad darbs ir ļoti daudz`** — confirm; `man ir ļoti daudz darba` may be more natural.
9. **`Man ir daudz jaunu skolēnu`** — genitive plural after `daudz`. Confirm, and confirm `skolēns` (pupil) over `students`.
10. **`Tas ir daudz darba, tiešām`** — confirm `tiešām` placement.
11. **`Mums jāmācās kopā`** — debitive of a reflexive verb. Confirm.
12. **`Tiekamies!`** — confirm this is the natural casual reply to `Uz redzēšanos`.
13. **`Oi`** — natural Latvian interjection, or is something else better?
