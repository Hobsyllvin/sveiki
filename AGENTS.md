## Lesson interface design

- Do not put individual sentences in cards or add borders or shadows around them; use whitespace to separate rows.
- Keep the palette restrained and avoid decorative icons.
- Treat interlinear pairs — Latvian above, gloss below — as the visual centrepiece. Supporting controls and metadata must remain quiet.
- All text, including muted gloss text, must meet WCAG AA contrast requirements.
